# Contributing

This repository is intended to remain a small, public-safe project showcase.

## Appropriate contributions

Contributions may improve:

- Plain-language project descriptions
- Accessibility and responsive behavior of the public page
- Typographical errors and broken relative links
- Public-safe diagrams or synthetic examples

## Do not contribute

Do not submit:

- Power Platform exports or unpacked solution source
- Operational formulas, prompts, flows, schemas, or deployment details
- Real forestry documents, extracted content, or screenshots
- Internal URLs, email addresses, tenant or report identifiers, credentials, or tokens
- Personal, tribal, controlled, or otherwise protected information

## Pull-request checks

Before opening a pull request:

1. Confirm that all examples are synthetic.
2. Search the changed files for identifiers, email addresses, URLs, and document content.
3. Verify that the public page works with keyboard navigation and at mobile width.
4. Complete the relevant items in [`PUBLIC_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md`](PUBLIC_RELEASE_CHECKLIST.md).
